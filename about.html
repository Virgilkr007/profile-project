About Me

Oluwakorede Adeogun — Software Developer & Aspiring AI Engineer

About Me

I am Oluwakorede Adeogun, a Computer Science student at the University of Ibadan, Nigeria, a software developer, technology enthusiast, and aspiring AI engineer. I am passionate about understanding how technology works and using it to solve meaningful problems. My journey in technology has taken me from software development and community building toward a deeper interest in Artificial Intelligence and intelligent systems.

My Christian faith is a central part of who I am. I am a committed Christian, and my faith shapes my values, how I approach my work, and how I understand purpose, service, excellence, and leadership. I believe that technology and knowledge should ultimately be used to create value and serve people.

I am also a Liverpool Football Club supporter and a passionate football fan. Supporting Liverpool is one of the things I enjoy outside technology, alongside learning, exploring ideas, and following developments in science and technology.

Academic Interests

My academic interests lie primarily in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, with particular interest in Large Language Models (LLMs), Small Language Models (SLMs), AI agents, agentic systems, natural language processing, machine learning, and intelligent software architectures.

I am especially interested in understanding how AI systems can reason, use tools, communicate with other systems, and accomplish complex tasks while remaining useful and controllable. I also enjoy studying the foundations behind these technologies, including algorithms, probability, statistics, software engineering, and system design.

Career Goals

My long-term goal is to become an AI engineer and eventually an expert in Artificial Intelligence, particularly in the development of intelligent and agentic systems. I want to build systems that are not simply applications of existing AI models, but thoughtfully engineered solutions capable of interacting with tools, data, software, and other intelligent systems.

I also hope to combine my background in software engineering with AI to build practical technologies that address real-world problems, particularly within Africa and other emerging technology ecosystems.

Leadership & Community

Beyond academics and software development, I have a strong interest in leadership, community building, education, and technology entrepreneurship. I have been involved in student technology communities and initiatives at the University of Ibadan, including the Stalwart Crest Student Innovation Club (SCSIC), where I have contributed to technical training, community coordination, and student engagement across different technology tracks.

I enjoy helping people learn, bringing people together around technology, and creating environments where others can develop their skills.

At the intersection of my faith, technology, education, and service, I am continually learning, building, and looking for ways to make meaningful contributions through the things I do.
